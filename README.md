# Scale Shift: The Inertia Slider

An arcade physics momentum platformer built with HTML5 Canvas, Tailwind CSS, and Web Audio API.

## Difficulties
- **🟢 EASY**: Includes **🛡️ 1 Energy Shield** per sector (absorbs a hazard collision, grants invulnerability & knockback to safety), slower platform crumbling (36 frames), easier blast wall smashing (0.72x threshold), extra coyote jump buffer (+4 frames), and relaxed par times (+35%).
- **🔵 NORMAL**: The authentic balanced kinetic challenge. Standard momentum physics, 18 frames crumble delay, standard wall breach thresholds, and standard speedrun par times.
- **🔴 HARD**: Hardcore precision mode. Zero error tolerance (instant hazard failure), lightning-fast platform crumbling (10 frames / ~160ms), strict kinetic breach speed requirement (1.25x threshold), and tight speedrun par times (-15%).

## Mechanics
- **TINY (0.4M)**: Floats effortlessly on updrafts, springboards high on Kinetic Bounce Pads, and squeezes through narrow conduits.
- **MEDIUM (1.0M)**: Balanced agility and traversal speed.
- **HEAVY (2.8M)**: Heavy mass with massive inertia. Smashes reinforced blast walls at velocity, presses heavy weight switches, and triggers collapse on crumbling bridges.

## Sectors (7 Full Campaigns)
1. **Sector 1: Mass Dynamics** - Introductory chamber introducing mass scaling, ducts, and blast walls.
2. **Sector 2: Kinetic Breach** - Kinetic speed runs, dual blast walls, and long hazard gaps.
3. **Sector 3: Momentum Slingshot** - High-velocity vertical bounce pads, narrow aerial ducts, and mid-air breach maneuvers.
4. **Sector 4: Gravitational Collapse** - Fragile crumbling platforms across laser chasms, dual gate switches, and the final breach portal.
5. **Sector 5: Sub-Zero Vector** - Cryogenic wind tunnels, multi-stage springboards, and laser grid timing.
6. **Sector 6: Orbital Centrifuge** - Radial speed tracks, high-altitude drops, and collapsing struts.
7. **Sector 7: Singularity Core** - The ultimate climax: multi-stage gauntlet culminating in a massive cliff dive kinetic breach!

## Controls
- **A / D / ← →**: Roll
- **W / ↑ / Space**: Jump (variable height)
- **1 / 2 / 3**: Shift to Tiny / Medium / Heavy
- **Shift / Mouse Wheel / Swipe Up-Down**: Cycle mass forms
- **R**: Restart sector
- **Click Sector Indicator (Top Header)**: Quick Sector Selector
- **Click Difficulty Badge (Top Header)**: Toggle Easy / Normal / Hard
